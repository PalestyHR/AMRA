About the project

Aircraft Maintenance
Record Assistant

AMRA combines language-model extraction, rule-based screening and human review. It reads current maintenance entries and related history, identifies review-relevant information, and links the findings to source quotations.

The workbench lets users inspect the evidence, add review notes and export the results. A prepared demo is available without an API key.

  1. Extract information
  2. Check evidence and screening rules
  3. Review and export

A student project at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.
